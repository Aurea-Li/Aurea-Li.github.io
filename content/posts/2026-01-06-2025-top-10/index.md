I didn't really put much thought into a top 10, I spent maybe 10 minutes combing through my archives and selecting ones that I liked. Here they are in no particular order! (The photos are a mixture of film and digital, can you tell the difference?)

{{< img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-9.jpg" alt="China morning market" >}}

The biggest trip I did this year was go to China. I did some street there and this is my favorite picture I took from that trip. I was walking in my grandma's neighborhood and stumbled upon a outdoor market in the morning. Apparently it was the time of year when garlic was being harvested, lots of vendors were selling it in large bunches.

{{< img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-1.jpg" alt="Harvesting shishito peppers" >}}

Speaking of harvest, here's one from our home garden when we were harvesting shishito peppers. I like how the arms form leading lines to the peppers, which is where the point of focus is.


{{< img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-8.jpg" alt="Neighborhood B&W 1" >}}

I enjoy taking walks around the neighborhood with my camera. Here's my favorite taken of the neighborhood.

{{< img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-4.jpg" alt="Wedding" >}}

One of my friends got married and I took the film camera with me to the wedding. Here is my favorite picture out of four rolls I shot! I snapped this at f/4 with a 50mm lens, right after the ceremony. I love their facial expressions; we were all given petals to throw at them as they walked past.

{{< img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-5.jpg" alt="Docks at West Seattle" >}}

{{< img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-6.jpg" alt="Ferry at the Waterfront" >}}

Both of these were taken in different locations along the Puget Sound. For the second, there was a small beach tucked in next to the waterfront that I quickly went to, took a picture and slinked away.

{{< img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-10.jpg" alt="Jefferson Park" >}}

One of the few clear days in winter I went to my favorite park in the city and took this shot. One of the reasons I love this park is because it has a giant community garden next to it.
