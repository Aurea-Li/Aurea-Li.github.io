Early 2025 was the year that I become "that person". By "that person" I mean the one who is always carrying around a camera and shoving it into your face. Luckily the people who know me and spend time with me tolerate this behavior... for now. When I look back at some of my favorite photos, a lot of them are portraits. But as much as I like those kinds of pictures it's not really a picture I can be super proud of, in a technical and compositional sense. I didn't really put much thought into my top 10, I spent maybe 10 minutes combing through my archives and selecting ones that I liked. Here they are in no particular order! (The photos are a mixture of film and digital, can you tell the difference?)

![China morning market](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-9.jpg?raw=true)

The biggest trip I did this year was go to China. I did some street there and this is my favorite picture I took from that trip. I was walking in my grandma's neighborhood and stumbled upon a outdoor market in the morning. Apparently it was the time of year when garlic was being harvested, lots of vendors were selling it in large bunches.

![Harvesting shisido peppers](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-1.jpg?raw=true)

Speaking of harvest, here's one from our home garden when we were harvesting shisido peppers. I like how the arms form leading lines to the peppers, which is where the point of focus is.


![Neighborhood B&W 1](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-8.jpg?raw=true)
![Neighborhood B&W 2](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-7.jpg?raw=true)

I enjoy taking walks around the neighborhood with my camera. I've almost always been walking around with the camera swinging from my hips, which has made me a little paranoid that I've gained a reputation from the neighborhood as "girl with camera". Oh well, it can't be helped! Here's two near where I live. The first was taken during early morning light, and the later was taken very recently, on Dec 31st. It was a foggy day.

![Wedding](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-4.jpg?raw=true)

One of my friends got married and I took the film camera with me to the wedding. Here is my favorite picture out of four rolls I shot! I snapped this at f/4 with a 50mm lens, right after the ceremony. I love their facial expressions; we were all given petals to throw at them as they walked past.

![Rattlesnake Ledge](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-2.jpg?raw=true)

This was taken during a hike on Rattlesnake Ledge. It was also pretty foggy that way. I feel lucky to live in an beautiful area with so much nature! The tradeoff is that the food scene here sucks though.

![Docks at West Seattle](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-5.jpg?raw=true)

![Ferry at the Waterfront](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-6.jpg?raw=true)

Both of these were taken in different locations along the Puget Sound. For the second, there was a small beach tucked in next to the waterfront that I quickly went to, took a picture and slinked away.

![Jefferson Park](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-10.jpg?raw=true)

A very recent picture. One of the few clear days in winter I went to my favorite park in the city and took this shot. One of the reasons I love this park is because it has a giant community garden next to it.

![Jefferson Park](https://github.com/Aurea-Li/image-store-2026/blob/main/2026-01-06-2025-top-10/untitled-3.jpg?raw=true)

Hah, I had to end it with a portrait because I'm a sucker for taking these! Taken at some greenhouse somewhere, I don't remember where.
