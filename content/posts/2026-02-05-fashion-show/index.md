I went with a small group of other photographers to an underground fashion show. It was a lot of fun to go to an event with a group of people. There were a lot of photographers at this event and we all had fun taking pictures together and chatting with one another.

This event was part of the monthly [Pioneer Square Art Walks](https://pioneersquare.org/art-walks/). It was my first time attending and I'm glad I went! I wish I had checked these out sooner. I've gotten into a habit after work of always staying home, and it's been detrimental to my mental health. So I'm glad I made an effort to go out into the city with people on a weekday night. I was surprised at how large the art walk was, there were lots of stores and people walking in and out of them; groups of people spilling onto the sidewalks to chat. Lots of people wearing cool and interesting things. Later when I was posting about it on instagram a friend reached out and said she was also there, unfortunately we didn't run into each other. Oh well, there's always next month!
