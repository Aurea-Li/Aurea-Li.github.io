There is something very relaxing about getting acupuncture. I love the 20 minutes lying there with needles in my body, nothing just me, the needles, and my thoughts. When I walk out of the clinic I feel very refreshed.

I've become more a fan of TCM (Traditional Chinese Medicine) stuff. I've been drinking hot water regularly for years and years, but recently I listened to a podcast where it was mentioned that it's more gentler for the qi of the food to steam it, and ever since then I've been making efforts to steam instead of microwave my leftovers. It takes more time and effort but I find it worth it especially for rice dishes. Sometimes if it's just a soup I'll throw it in the microwave. I have two woks at home, one that is rusted that we only use for steaming, and the other for stir frying.
