I find going outside to be a panacea of sorts. The sun is magnitudes of times greater than the brightest indoor lighting, even when it's overcast (as it frequently is here). Going outside wakes me up and lets myself get closer to nature, which has healing qualities. Anyways, I'm probably preaching to the choir here...
